About us

Science belongs to everyone

We're a community of researchers, writers, educators, and curious minds working to close the gap between the lab and the living room. Founded on July 28, 2026, SciComm Initiative runs a blog, a podcast, monthly in-person events, and training workshops — all centred on the belief that good science communication makes society smarter and more resilient.

What we believe
🔬
Accuracy over virality
We never sacrifice scientific accuracy for a more exciting headline. When findings are uncertain, we say so.
🗣️
Dialogue, not broadcast
Science communication works best as a conversation. We create spaces where the public shapes the agenda too.
🌍
Inclusion and access
Most of our events and content are free. We work to platform voices that are underrepresented in science media.
✨
Craft matters
Good science communication is good writing, good storytelling, good listening. We take the craft seriously.
